Oxodonia Ltd – Accessibility Policy

Effective Date: June 20, 2025

Our Commitment to Accessibility Oxodonia Ltd is committed to making our website and digital services accessible to as many people as possible, regardless of any disability or impairment. We believe everyone should be able to use our website with ease. In developing and maintaining our site, we aspire to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level – a globally recognized standard for web accessibility whoisaccessible.com . By aiming for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, we align with the requirements and best practices adopted in the UK, especially those expected of public sector digital services. We understand that accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time task. We are continually working to improve the user experience for all visitors and to apply the relevant accessibility standards. Our goal is to ensure that all users, including those using assistive technologies (such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, voice recognition software, etc.), can effectively use our site. Oxodonia Ltd also affirms its commitment to the principles of the Equality Act 2010, which requires organizations to make reasonable adjustments for disabled individuals to avoid disadvantage whoisaccessible.com . We strive to ensure that our digital content and services do not discriminate against any user and are accessible in line with the spirit of this law. In practice, this means we attempt to design and build our website in a way that does not exclude people because of their disabilities and we take accessibility into account in new features and updates. Compliance Status We are working toward full compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. We have evaluated our website’s accessibility and believe that many of the WCAG 2.1 AA success criteria are being met. For instance: Text content is written in plain language as much as possible, to be easy to understand. Our site’s layout and colors are chosen to have sufficient contrast and readability. The website is navigable via keyboard only (for users who cannot use a mouse or touchscreen). Images on our site include alternative text descriptions where appropriate, so that users with visual impairments can understand their content. We avoid using media or animations that flash rapidly, to reduce the risk of triggering seizures or causing discomfort. Our site is responsive and works with screen magnification and on different screen sizes (desktop, tablet, mobile). While we aim for AA compliance, we acknowledge there might be some areas that need improvement. For example, if any PDF documents or third-party content on our site are not fully accessible, we are looking into providing accessible alternatives. As of the date of this statement, we are not aware of major accessibility issues, but there may be minor issues that we haven’t yet identified. If you encounter any specific accessibility issue on our site, please let us know – your feedback is valuable and will help us improve. Enforcement Procedure We aim to resolve all accessibility issues directly with users who contact us. However, if you have contacted us about an accessibility concern and you are not satisfied with our response or the outcome, further help is available. The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) is responsible for enforcing the Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations (for public sector websites) and the Equality Act 2010 in the UK. Since Oxodonia Ltd is a private company, those specific regulations may not directly apply to us; regardless, we voluntarily align with those standards. If you are unhappy with our response to your accessibility feedback, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS) for guidance. The EASS is an independent advice service that can provide information and support on discrimination and human rights issues, including accessibility of websites gov.uk . (For users in Northern Ireland, if applicable: you may contact the Equalities Commission for Northern Ireland.) We truly hope it never reaches this stage, as we are dedicated to working with users to fix issues wherever possible. Technical Information About This Website’s Accessibility Oxodonia Ltd is committed to maintaining an accessible website. This section outlines the technical status of www.oxodonia.co.uk: Compliance Status: We are partially compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA standards. “Partially compliant” means that while most of the key aspects of the guidelines are met, there may be some criteria that have not been fully addressed yet. We consider this an ongoing journey toward full compliance. Non-Accessible Content: We strive to identify and fix any parts of our content that are not accessible. At this time, we have not identified significant non-compliances. However, some third-party content (for example, an embedded map or external document) might not fully meet accessibility standards. If such content is critical to your use of our site and is not accessible to you, contact us and we will try to assist or provide an accessible alternative. Technologies Used: The website relies on modern web technologies (HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript) which we ensure are used in accordance with accessibility best practices. The site should be compatible with current versions of major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and with assistive technologies like screen readers (tested with VoiceOver, NVDA, or JAWS on common platforms). Features for Accessibility: We include features such as skip navigation links, ARIA labels for interactive elements, and text equivalents for media where possible. Headings and page structures are organized in a logical manner to aid navigation. Ongoing Efforts and Improvements We recognize that accessibility needs can change and that standards evolve. Oxodonia Ltd is committed to continually reviewing and improving our site’s accessibility. Some steps we either have taken or are planning to take include: Regular Audits: Periodically auditing our site using automated accessibility testing tools and manual testing (including using screen readers and keyboard navigation) to catch any issues. Team Training: Ensuring our development and content team members are trained in accessibility principles so that new content or features are built with accessibility in mind from the start. User Testing: When possible, seeking feedback from users with disabilities to learn where real-world improvements can be made. Updates to Compliance Standards: Staying informed about updates to accessibility guidelines or regulations (such as any new WCAG versions or changes in UK law) so that we remain compliant. For example, if WCAG 2.2 or 3.0 becomes widely adopted or expected, we will aim to accommodate the new recommendations. Through these efforts, we aim to not only meet the required standards but to exceed them where we can, providing a positive experience for all users. Preparation of this Accessibility Statement This Accessibility Statement was prepared on the basis of our ongoing self-assessment and commitment to accessibility. It was last updated on 21 June 2025. We initially evaluated our site’s accessibility during its design and development phases and have continued to test it after launch. The testing included internal reviews of representative pages and elements of the site, using tools like browser developer accessibility inspectors and accessibility testing software, as well as manual checks (e.g., trying to navigate via keyboard only). We have also considered the guidelines provided by the UK Government for public sector accessibility statements to ensure our statement covers the necessary information and user needs. We plan to review this statement at least annually, or more frequently if significant changes are made to our website that could impact its accessibility.
